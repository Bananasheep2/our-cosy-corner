First launch — the one-time warning

Because this is a brand-new independent app, macOS and Windows show an extra prompt the first time. Nothing is wrong. Here's exactly what to click.

macOS 15 (Sequoia)

Open the app — it will say it's blocked, so click Done. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the "Our Cosy Corner was blocked" notice, click Open Anyway, and confirm.

macOS 13 / 14

Right-click the app → Open → Open. You only need to do this once.

Windows

SmartScreen may say the app is unrecognized — click More info, then Run anyway.

If macOS says the app "is damaged"

Open Terminal and run this, then relaunch:

xattr -cr "/Applications/Our Cosy Corner.app"

None of this means anything is wrong — Our Cosy Corner just isn't notarized with Apple (or signed with a paid Windows certificate) yet, so both systems show an extra warning for a brand-new, independently-made app.

“

I created Our Cosy Corner because I wanted a shared dashboard with my boyfriend — somewhere we could plan our future together, our own individual futures, our resumes, work, life, school, and all the in-between things that matter to us.

The name came from the feeling I wanted it to have: a cosy, small space for the two of us to collaborate without being interrupted by ads, paywalls, or noise. Just a quiet corner that belongs to us.

Our Cosy Corner is made to hold the practical things and the tender things side by side — the plan for next year, the link to a CV, the photo from last weekend, and the love letter you are not ready to send yet.
