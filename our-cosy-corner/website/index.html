First launch

This build isn't notarized or code-signed yet, so your operating system will show a one-time warning the first time you open it. This is expected — here's how to get past it.

macOS 15 Sequoia

  1. Double-click the app. macOS will block it and show a warning — dismiss that dialog.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the message that says "Our Cosy Corner" was blocked, and click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm in the follow-up prompt, and the app will open from then on.

macOS 13 / 14

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) the app.
  2. Choose Open from the menu.
  3. Click Open again on the confirmation dialog.

"App is damaged and can't be opened"

This message is macOS being overly cautious about an unsigned app, not an actual problem with the download. Open Terminal and run:

xattr -cr "/Applications/Our Cosy Corner.app"

Then relaunch the app as usual.

Windows

  1. You'll see a blue "Windows protected your PC" SmartScreen notice.
  2. Click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.
